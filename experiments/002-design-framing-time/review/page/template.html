Data Commons Spotlight · Experiment 002

Banner review

Connecting to saved reviews

One verdict per banner. Open a banner, choose Looks right, Needs a change or Don't post, tick what the note is about, and say what should change. Each banner is reviewed with its X, LinkedIn and Bluesky captions and its alt text. For anything that applies to many banners at once, add a general note instead of repeating it.

What happens next. I read every saved review, fix what you flag in the scripts, rebuild with build_all.py, push to the batch branch and reply on the banner. A rebuilt banner comes back as "Rebuilt, review again".

What a verdict is not. It is a note to me, not the approval: merging the batch PR stays the approval. Don't post is logged as a deviation in the preregistration; assignment.csv stays as drawn.

General notes

For comments that apply across banners: a wording habit, a chart rule, a design choice. Choose which banners the note covers; I apply it wherever it fits, rebuild, and reply under the note.
