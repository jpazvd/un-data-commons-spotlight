Field notes · Data Commons Spotlight 01 SDG 5.3.1 · Child marriage · 23 Oct 2026
18.5%

of women aged 20–24 worldwide were married or in a union before age 18 (2025).

Down from 24.2% in 2005, about 0.3 points a year. Ending it by 2030 needs 3.7 points a year, nearly 13 times faster.

My note

Three of the six highest rates rest on surveys from 2015 or earlier. Before we can say how fast these countries are moving, we need newer data.
