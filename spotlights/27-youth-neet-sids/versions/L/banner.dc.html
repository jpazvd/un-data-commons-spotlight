UN Data Commons Spotlight SDG 8.6.1 · youth NEET rate

Since 2005, youth NEET rate in Small Island Developing States (SIDS) has fallen 2.3 points

From 23.4% in 2005 to 21.1% in 2025: 0.1 points a year.

Data: ILO via UN SDG Global Database, series SL_TLF_NEET, SL_TLF_NEET_19ICLS, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: NEET, not in education, employment or training; SIDS, small island developing states.
