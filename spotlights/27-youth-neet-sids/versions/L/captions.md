# Spotlight 27-L · Youth NEET, Small island developing states (SIDS), by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2005, youth NEET rate in Small Island Developing States (SIDS) has fallen 2.3 points. From 23.4% in 2005 to 21.1% in 2025: 0.1 points a year.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.

What has moved youth NEET rate most in the places you work in, and how do you know?

Method: published series, 2005 to 2025; change in percentage points; series SL_TLF_NEET, which the source labels the previous definition.

Data: ILO, UN SDG Global Database (series SL_TLF_NEET, SL_TLF_NEET_19ICLS, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
