UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 6.2.1 · safely managed sanitation

In LDCs, safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 12.2 points a year

So far it has risen 0.5 points a year, from 14.3% in 2000 to 27.0% in 2024.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_SAN_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries.
