# Spotlight 09-L · Safely managed sanitation, Least developed countries (LDCs), by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, safely managed sanitation in Least developed countries (LDCs) has risen 12.7 points. From 14.3% in 2000 to 27.0% in 2024: 0.5 points a year.

In Least developed countries (LDCs), safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 12.2 points a year, against 0.5 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
