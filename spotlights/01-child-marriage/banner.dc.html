UN System Data Commons · Spotlight 01 SDG 5.3.1 · Children & women · 23 Oct 2026

Nearly 1 in 5 women aged 20–24 was married before turning 18.

Women aged 20–24 first married or in union before 18: 24.2% in 2005 → 18.5% in 2025. That is about 0.3 points a year. Ending child marriage by 2030 would take roughly 3.7 points a year, some 13 times the pace of the last two decades.
