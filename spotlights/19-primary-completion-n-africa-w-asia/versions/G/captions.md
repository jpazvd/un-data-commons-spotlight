# Spotlight 19-G · School completion, Northern Africa and Western Asia, by wealth: post captions

## LinkedIn

Primary completion was lower for the poorest fifth in 12 of 14 countries in N. Africa and W. Asia. Latest surveys with both groups, 2004 to 2023; median gap 8.7 points.

In Northern Africa and Western Asia, every child completing primary school by 2030 needs the rate to rise 1.7 points a year, against 0.7 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 3 of the 14 countries the two groups are less than one point apart.

Where have you seen the gap in primary completion between the poorest fifth and the richest fifth close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (2004 to 2023); counts, not an average.

Data: UNESCO UIS, UN SDG Global Database (series SE_TOT_CPLR, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
