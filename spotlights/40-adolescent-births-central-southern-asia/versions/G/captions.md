# Spotlight 40-G · Adolescent birth rate, Central and Southern Asia, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Adolescent birth rate was higher in rural areas in 10 of 11 countries in Central and S. Asia. Latest surveys with both groups, 1996 to 2019; median gap 17.1 per 1,000.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 1 of the 11 countries the two groups are less than 1 per 1,000 apart.

Where have you seen the gap in adolescent birth rate between rural areas and urban areas close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (1996 to 2019); counts, not an average.

Data: WHO Global Health Observatory, UN System Data Commons (indicator FERT_ADO, extracted 1 Nov 2026).
