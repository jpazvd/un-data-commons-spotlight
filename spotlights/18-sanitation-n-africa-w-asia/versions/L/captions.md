## LinkedIn

Since 2000, safely managed sanitation in Northern Africa and Western Asia has risen 21.3 points. From 39.4% in 2000 to 60.7% in 2024: 0.9 points a year.

In Northern Africa and Western Asia, safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 6.5 points a year, against 0.9 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
