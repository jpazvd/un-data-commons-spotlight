Maternal mortality below 70 by 2030 means cutting 18.2 a year; since 2000, 5.7 a year

The maternal mortality ratio (MMR) is defined as the number of maternal deaths during a given time period per 100,000 live births during the same time period.
MMEIG estimates, world series 2000 to 2023; the shaded band to 2030 holds projections, not data.
