# Spotlight 05-L · Safely managed drinking water, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Safely managed drinking water: up 13.0 points in LDCs, 10.6 in LLDCs and 4.0 in SIDS since 2000. LDCs: from 25.8% in 2000 to 38.8% in 2024. World: from 61.3% to 73.7%.

Safely managed drinking water for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 7.1 to 10.5 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in safely managed drinking water in Least developed countries, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2000 to 2024.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_H2O_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
