UN Data Commons Spotlight SDG 6.1.1 · safely managed drinking water

Safely managed water for all by 2030 needs a rise of 7.1 to 10.5 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS

In LLDCs it has risen 0.4 points a year, from 26.3% in 2000 to 36.9% in 2024.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_H2O_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
