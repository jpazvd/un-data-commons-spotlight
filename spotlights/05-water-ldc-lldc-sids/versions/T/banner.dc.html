UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 6.1.1 · safely managed drinking water

Safely managed water for all by 2030 needs a rise of 7.1 to 10.5 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs, SIDS

Proportion of the population using drinking water from an improved source that is accessible on premises, available when needed and free from contamination
Linear pace on published aggregates, from 2000 for LLDCs.
Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_H2O_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub · CC BY 4.0
Definition: UNICEF Reference Data Manager, indicator WS_PPL_W-SM
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
