# Spotlight 05-T · Safely managed drinking water, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Safely managed water for all by 2030 needs a rise of 7.1 to 10.5 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS. In LLDCs it has risen 0.4 points a year, from 26.3% in 2000 to 36.9% in 2024.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed. The paces are straight lines, which is simple on purpose.

Which LLDCs have moved safely managed drinking water fastest, and how?

Method: linear, points per year, from each place's first published year to its latest, and from there to 2030; published aggregates.
