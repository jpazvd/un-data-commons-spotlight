# Spotlight 11-L · Pre-primary participation, SDG regions compared, by sex: post captions

## LinkedIn

Northern Africa and Western Asia: pre-primary participation up 23.2 points since 2005. N. Africa and W. Asia: from 28.8% in 2005 to 52.0% in 2024. World: from 65.3% to 75.0%.

Every child in organized learning before primary by 2030 needs participation in Northern Africa and Western Asia to rise 8.0 points a year, against 1.0 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in pre-primary participation in Northern Africa and Western Asia, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2005 to 2024; left out because their series start later: Central and S. Asia, Oceania (excl. Aus., NZ).

Data: UNESCO UIS, UN SDG Global Database (series SE_PRE_PARTN, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
