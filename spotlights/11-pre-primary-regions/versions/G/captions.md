## LinkedIn

Pre-primary participation: the widest gap between girls and boys is 1.2 points, in N. Africa and W. Asia. Northern Africa and Western Asia: 52.6% for girls, 51.4% for boys, 2024.

Every child in organized learning before primary by 2030 needs participation in Northern Africa and Western Asia to rise 8.0 points a year, against 1.0 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates by group, not averages I computed from country values.

Where have you seen the gap in pre-primary participation between girls and boys close, and how was it measured?

Method: published aggregates by group, each region's latest year with both groups (2017 to 2024).
