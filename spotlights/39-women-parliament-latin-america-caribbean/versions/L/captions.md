# Spotlight 39-L · Women in national parliaments, Latin America and the Caribbean: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, women in parliament in Latin America and the Caribbean has risen 21.4 points. From 15.2% in 2000 to 36.6% in 2026: 0.8 points a year.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
