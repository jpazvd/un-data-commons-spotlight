UN Data Commons Spotlight SDG 3.1.2 · skilled birth attendance

Worldwide in 2025: 87.2% of births attended by skilled health personnel

From 86.7% in SIDS to 76.0% in LLDCs.

Data: UNICEF and WHO via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_STA_BRTC, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: SIDS, small island developing states; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; LDCs, least developed countries.
