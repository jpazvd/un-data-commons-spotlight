UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 3.1.2 · skilled birth attendance

Since 2000, skilled birth attendance has risen 46.7 points in LDCs, 43.6 in LLDCs and 18.3 in SIDS

LDCs: from 29.9% in 2000 to 76.6% in 2025. World: from 60.7% to 87.2%.

Data: UNICEF and WHO via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_STA_BRTC, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
