UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 3.1.2 · skilled birth attendance

Skilled birth attendance: up 46.7 points in LDCs, 43.6 in LLDCs and 18.3 in SIDS since 2000

Proportion of births attended by skilled health personnel (generally doctors, nurses or midwives but can refer to other health professionals providing childbirth care) is the proportion of childbirths attended by skilled health personnel.
Published aggregates, 2000 to 2025.
Data: UNICEF and WHO via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_STA_BRTC, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub · CC BY 4.0
Definition: UNICEF Reference Data Manager, indicator MNCH_SAB
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
