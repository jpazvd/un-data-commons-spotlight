# Spotlight 16-T · Safely managed drinking water, World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Safely managed drinking water for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 4.4 points a year. So far it has risen 0.5 points a year, from 61.3% in 2000 to 73.7% in 2024.

That is 8.4 times the pace of the last 24 years.

A statistician's note: both paces are straight lines, the observed one over the whole series and the required one from the latest estimate to the target. The multiple between them changes with the base year; the required pace does not.

Where has safely managed drinking water moved at anything like 4.4 points a year, and what made it possible?

Method: linear, points per year, 2000 to 2024 for the trend and 2024 to 2030 for the target.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_H2O_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
