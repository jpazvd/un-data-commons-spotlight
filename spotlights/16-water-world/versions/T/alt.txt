Safely managed drinking water for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 4.4 points a year. So far it has risen 0.5 points a year, from 61.3% in 2000 to 73.7% in 2024. People using safely managed drinking water, the world: 61.3% in 2000, 73.7% in 2024. At that pace about 76.9% in 2030; reaching 100% needs 4.4 points a year.
