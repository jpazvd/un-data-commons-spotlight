# Spotlight 07-T · Child marriage before 18, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Ending child marriage by 2030 needs the share to fall 4.4 to 7.0 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS. In LDCs it has fallen 0.6 points a year, from 46.5% in 2005 to 35.0% in 2025.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed. The paces are straight lines, which is simple on purpose.

Which LDCs have moved child marriage fastest, and how?

Method: linear, points per year, from each place's first published year to its latest, and from there to 2030; published aggregates.

Data: UNICEF, UN SDG Global Database (series SP_DYN_MRBF18, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
