Ending child marriage by 2030 needs the share to fall 4.4 to 7.0 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS. In LDCs it has fallen 0.6 points a year, from 46.5% in 2005 to 35.0% in 2025. LDCs: 0.6 a year observed, 7.0 needed; LLDCs: 0.4 a year observed, 6.3 needed; SIDS: 0.2 a year observed, 4.4 needed; World: 0.3 a year observed, 3.7 needed
