UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 5.3.1 · child marriage

Since 2005, child marriage has fallen 11.5 points in LDCs, 8.7 in LLDCs and 3.7 in SIDS

LDCs: from 46.5% in 2005 to 35.0% in 2025. World: from 24.2% to 18.5%.

Data: UNICEF via UN SDG Global Database, series SP_DYN_MRBF18, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
