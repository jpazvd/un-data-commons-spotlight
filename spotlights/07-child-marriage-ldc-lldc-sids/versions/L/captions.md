# Spotlight 07-L · Child marriage before 18, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2005, child marriage has fallen 11.5 points in LDCs, 8.7 in LLDCs and 3.7 in SIDS. LDCs: from 46.5% in 2005 to 35.0% in 2025. World: from 24.2% to 18.5%.

Ending child marriage by 2030 needs the share to fall 4.4 to 7.0 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in child marriage in Least developed countries, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2005 to 2025.

Data: UNICEF, UN SDG Global Database (series SP_DYN_MRBF18, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
