# Spotlight 07-D · Child marriage before 18, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Data on child marriage cover 44 of 44 LDCs, 31 of 32 LLDCs and 27 of 39 SIDS. Countries with any value; for 44 of the 79 with data, the latest is from 2020 or earlier.

Ending child marriage by 2030 needs the share to fall 4.4 to 7.0 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS.

A statistician's note: "latest value" means each country's most recent estimate, so values sit in different years. A gap here is a gap in the data, not a judgement on any statistical office.

Which countries have found a way to measure child marriage more often, and what made it possible?
