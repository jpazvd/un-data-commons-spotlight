# Spotlight 22-T · Safely managed sanitation, Eastern and South-eastern Asia: post captions

## LinkedIn

In E. and S.-E. Asia, safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 6.0 points a year. So far it has risen 1.8 points a year, from 19.8% in 2000 to 64.1% in 2024.

That is 3.2 times the pace of the last 24 years.

A statistician's note: both paces are straight lines, the observed one over the whole series and the required one from the latest estimate to the target. The multiple between them changes with the base year; the required pace does not.

Where has safely managed sanitation moved at anything like 6.0 points a year, and what made it possible?

Method: linear, points per year, 2000 to 2024 for the trend and 2024 to 2030 for the target.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_SAN_SAFE, extracted 4 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
