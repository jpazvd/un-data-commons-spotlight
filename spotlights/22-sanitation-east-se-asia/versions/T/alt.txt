In E. and S.-E. Asia, safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 6.0 points a year. So far it has risen 1.8 points a year, from 19.8% in 2000 to 64.1% in 2024. People using safely managed sanitation, Eastern and South-eastern Asia: 19.8% in 2000, 64.1% in 2024. At that pace about 75.2% in 2030; reaching 100% needs 6.0 points a year.
