# Spotlight 22-L · Safely managed sanitation, Eastern and South-eastern Asia: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, safely managed sanitation in Eastern and South-eastern Asia has risen 44.3 points. From 19.8% in 2000 to 64.1% in 2024: 1.8 points a year.

In Eastern and South-eastern Asia, safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 6.0 points a year, against 1.8 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
