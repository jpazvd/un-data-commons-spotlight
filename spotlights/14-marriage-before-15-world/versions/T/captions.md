# Spotlight 14-T · Child marriage before 15, World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Ending marriage before 15 by 2030 needs the share to fall 0.8 points a year. So far it has fallen 0.2 points a year, from 7.8% in 2005 to 4.2% in 2025.

That is 4.6 times the pace of the last 20 years.

A statistician's note: both paces are straight lines, the observed one over the whole series and the required one from the latest estimate to the target. The multiple between them changes with the base year; the required pace does not.

Where has marriage before 15 moved at anything like 0.8 points a year, and what made it possible?

Method: linear, points per year, 2005 to 2025 for the trend and 2025 to 2030 for the target.

Data: UNICEF, UN SDG Global Database (series SP_DYN_MRBF15, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
