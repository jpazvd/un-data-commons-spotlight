# Spotlight 14-D · Child marriage before 15, World: post captions

## LinkedIn

140 countries and areas have data on marriage before 15; for 81, the latest is from 2020 or earlier. Latest values 2006 to 2025; median year 2019.

Ending marriage before 15 by 2030 needs the share to fall 0.8 points a year, against 0.2 points a year since 2005.

A statistician's note: "latest value" means each country's most recent estimate, so values sit in different years and are not directly comparable. A gap here is a gap in the data, not a judgement on any statistical office.

Which countries have found a way to measure marriage before 15 more often, and what made it possible?
