# Spotlight 23-L · Family planning demand satisfied (modern methods), LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Demand met with modern methods: up 21.6 points in LDCs, 20.8 in LLDCs and 1.5 in SIDS since 2000. LDCs: from 38.5% in 2000 to 60.1% in 2025. World: from 73.7% to 77.2%.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in demand met with modern methods in Least developed countries, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2000 to 2025.

Data: UN DESA Population Division and UNFPA, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_FPL_MTMM, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
