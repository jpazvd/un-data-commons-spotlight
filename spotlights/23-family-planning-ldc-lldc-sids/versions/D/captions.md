# Spotlight 23-D · Family planning demand satisfied (modern methods), LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Data on demand met with modern methods cover 43 of 44 LDCs, 32 of 32 LLDCs and 27 of 39 SIDS. Countries with any value; for 61 of the 79 with data, the latest is from 2020 or earlier.

A statistician's note: "latest value" means each country's most recent estimate, so values sit in different years. A gap here is a gap in the data, not a judgement on any statistical office.

Which countries have found a way to measure demand met with modern methods more often, and what made it possible?

Method: countries and areas with any value in the database, by membership, each counted once; 2020 or earlier counts as not recent.

Data: UN DESA Population Division and UNFPA, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_FPL_MTMM, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
