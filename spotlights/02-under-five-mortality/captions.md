# Spotlight 02 · Under-five mortality (SDG 3.2.1): post captions

## LinkedIn

66 of 199 countries and areas had more than 25 deaths before age five per 1,000 live births in 2024.

Worldwide, under-five mortality fell from 76.7 to 37.4 deaths per 1,000 live births between 2000 and 2024. But SDG target 3.2 is set country by country: every country at 25 or below by 2030. The highest rates in 2024 were in Nigeria (115.6), Niger (110.7), Somalia (101.1), Chad (97.3), South Sudan (96.7) and Guinea (92.1).

A statistician's note: these are UN IGME estimates, all for 2024, modelled from surveys, censuses and civil registration. Where births and deaths are not fully registered, I am reading a trend fitted to several quality-assessed surveys and censuses, not a count, and it comes with an uncertainty interval. That is why a country close to the line of 25 cannot be called on or off track with confidence.

In the countries you work with, what has done most to bring under-five deaths below 25 per 1,000, and how did you know it was working?

Method: count of countries and areas whose latest estimate (2024) is above 25 deaths per 1,000 live births, the SDG 3.2 per-country threshold; world series 2000 to 2024.

Data: UN Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation (UN IGME), via the UN SDG Global Database (indicator 3.2.1) and the UN System Data Commons.
