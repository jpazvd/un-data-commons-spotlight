UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 6.2.1 · safely managed sanitation

Since 2000, safely managed sanitation has risen 12.7 points in LDCs, 8.8 in LLDCs and 2.3 in SIDS

LDCs: from 14.3% in 2000 to 27.0% in 2024. World: from 31.3% to 58.5%.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_SAN_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
