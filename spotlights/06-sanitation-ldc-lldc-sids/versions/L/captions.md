# Spotlight 06-L · Safely managed sanitation, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, safely managed sanitation has risen 12.7 points in LDCs, 8.8 in LLDCs and 2.3 in SIDS. LDCs: from 14.3% in 2000 to 27.0% in 2024. World: from 31.3% to 58.5%.

Safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 9.7 to 12.2 points a year in LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in safely managed sanitation in Least developed countries, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2000 to 2024.

Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_SAN_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
