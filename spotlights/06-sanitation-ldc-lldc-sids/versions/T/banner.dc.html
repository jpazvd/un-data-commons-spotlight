UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 6.2.1 · safely managed sanitation

Safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs a rise of 9.7 to 12.2 points a year

Proportion of population using an improved sanitation facility that is not shared with other households, from which excreta are safely disposed of in situ or removed and treated off-site
Linear pace on published aggregates, from 2000 for LDCs.
Data: WHO/UNICEF JMP via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_SAN_SAFE, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub · CC BY 4.0
Definition: UNICEF Reference Data Manager, indicator WS_PPL_S-SM
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
