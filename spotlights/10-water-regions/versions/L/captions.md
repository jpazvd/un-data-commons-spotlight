Central and Southern Asia: safely managed drinking water up 29.2 points since 2000. Central and S. Asia: from 47.8% in 2000 to 77.0% in 2024. World: from 61.3% to 73.7%.

Safely managed drinking water for all by 2030 needs coverage in Sub-Saharan Africa to rise 11.4 points a year, against 0.6 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.
