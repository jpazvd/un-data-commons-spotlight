Central and Southern Asia: child marriage down 22.7 points since 2005. Central and S. Asia: from 47.3% in 2005 to 24.6% in 2025. World: from 24.2% to 18.5%.

Ending child marriage by 2030 needs the share in Sub-Saharan Africa to fall 6.1 points a year, against 0.4 points a year since 2005.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

Method: published aggregates, 2005 to 2025.
