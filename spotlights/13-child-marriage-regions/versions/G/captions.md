# Spotlight 13-G · Child marriage before 18, SDG regions compared, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

In 107 of 119 countries, child marriage was higher in rural than in urban areas in the latest survey. Latest surveys with both groups, 2006 to 2025; median gap 10.1 points.

Ending child marriage by 2030 needs the share in Sub-Saharan Africa to fall 6.1 points a year, against 0.4 points a year since 2005.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 13 of the 119 countries the two groups are less than one point apart.

Where have you seen the gap in child marriage between rural areas and urban areas close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (2006 to 2025); counts, not an average.

Data: UNICEF Data Warehouse, UN System Data Commons (indicator PT_MRD_U18, extracted 1 Nov 2026).

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
