UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 2.2.3 · anaemia in women

Worldwide in 2023: 30.7% of women aged 15 to 49 with anaemia

From 37.2% in LDCs to 28.7% in SIDS.

Data: WHO via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_STA_ANEM, SH_STA_ANEM_NPRG, SH_STA_ANEM_PREG, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; SIDS, small island developing states; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries.
