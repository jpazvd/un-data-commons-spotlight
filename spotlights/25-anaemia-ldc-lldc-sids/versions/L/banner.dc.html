UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 2.2.3 · anaemia in women

Since 2000, anaemia in women has fallen 3.6 points in LDCs, 3.1 in LLDCs and 1.5 in SIDS

LDCs: from 40.8% in 2000 to 37.2% in 2023. World: from 29.3% to 30.7%.

Data: WHO via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_STA_ANEM, SH_STA_ANEM_NPRG, SH_STA_ANEM_PREG, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LDCs, least developed countries; LLDCs, landlocked developing countries; SIDS, small island developing states.
