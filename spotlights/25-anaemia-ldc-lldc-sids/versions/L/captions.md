# Spotlight 25-L · Anaemia in women 15-49, LDCs, LLDCs and SIDS compared with the World: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, anaemia in women has fallen 3.6 points in LDCs, 3.1 in LLDCs and 1.5 in SIDS. LDCs: from 40.8% in 2000 to 37.2% in 2023. World: from 29.3% to 30.7%.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in anaemia in women in Least developed countries, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2000 to 2023.

Data: WHO, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_STA_ANEM, SH_STA_ANEM_NPRG, SH_STA_ANEM_PREG, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
