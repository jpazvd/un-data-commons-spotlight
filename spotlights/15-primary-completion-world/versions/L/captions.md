Since 2000, primary completion has risen 10.7 points. From 77.3% in 2000 to 88.0% in 2024: 0.4 points a year.

Every child completing primary school by 2030 needs the rate to rise 2.0 points a year, against 0.4 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
