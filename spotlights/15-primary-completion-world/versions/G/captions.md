# Spotlight 15-G · School completion, World, by wealth: post captions

## LinkedIn

In 117 of 122 countries, primary completion was lower among the poorest fifth than the richest. Latest surveys with both groups, 2000 to 2024; median gap 12.6 points.

Every child completing primary school by 2030 needs the rate to rise 2.0 points a year, against 0.4 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 17 of the 122 countries the two groups are less than one point apart.

Where have you seen the gap in primary completion between the poorest fifth and the richest fifth close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (2000 to 2024); counts, not an average.

Data: UNESCO UIS, UN SDG Global Database (series SE_TOT_CPLR, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
