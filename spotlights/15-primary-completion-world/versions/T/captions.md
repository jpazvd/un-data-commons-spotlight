# Spotlight 15-T · School completion, World, by wealth: post captions

## LinkedIn

Primary completion for all by 2030 needs the rate to rise 2.0 points a year, 4.5 times its pace since 2000. So far it has risen 0.4 points a year, from 77.3% in 2000 to 88.0% in 2024.

That is 4.5 times the pace of the last 24 years.

A statistician's note: both paces are straight lines, the observed one over the whole series and the required one from the latest estimate to the target. The multiple between them changes with the base year; the required pace does not.

Where has primary completion moved at anything like 2.0 points a year, and what made it possible?

Method: linear, points per year, 2000 to 2024 for the trend and 2024 to 2030 for the target.

Data: UNESCO UIS, UN SDG Global Database (series SE_TOT_CPLR, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
