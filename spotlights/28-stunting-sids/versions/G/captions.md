# Spotlight 28-G · Stunting, Small island developing states (SIDS), by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

In 20 of 25 SIDS, stunting was higher in rural than in urban areas in the latest survey. Latest surveys with both groups, 2010 to 2022; median gap 2.5 points.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 6 of the 25 countries the two groups are less than one point apart.

Where have you seen the gap in stunting between rural areas and urban areas close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (2010 to 2022); counts, not an average.

Data: UNICEF Data Warehouse, UN System Data Commons (indicator NT_ANT_HAZ_NE2, extracted 1 Nov 2026).

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
