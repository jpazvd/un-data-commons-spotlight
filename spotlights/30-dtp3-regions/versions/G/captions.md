# Spotlight 30-G · DTP3 immunization, SDG regions compared, by wealth: post captions

## LinkedIn

In 85 of 107 countries, DTP3 coverage was lower among the poorest fifth than the richest. Latest surveys with both groups, 1996 to 2020; median gap 9.5 points.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 9 of the 107 countries the two groups are less than one point apart.

Where have you seen the gap in DTP3 coverage between the poorest fifth and the richest fifth close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (1996 to 2020); counts, not an average.

Data: WHO Global Health Observatory, UN System Data Commons (indicator IMM_COV_SH, extracted 1 Nov 2026).
