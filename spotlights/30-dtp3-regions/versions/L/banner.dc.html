UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 3.b.1 · DTP3 coverage

Since 2000, Central and Southern Asia has raised DTP3 coverage by 30 points, the most of any region

Central and S. Asia: from 62% in 2000 to 92% in 2024. World: from 72% to 85%.

Data: WHO/UNICEF WUENIC via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_ACS_DTP3, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: DTP3, third dose of diphtheria, tetanus and pertussis vaccine.
