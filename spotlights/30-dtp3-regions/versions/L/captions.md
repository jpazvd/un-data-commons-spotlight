# Spotlight 30-L · DTP3 immunization, SDG regions compared, by wealth: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, Central and Southern Asia has raised DTP3 coverage by 30 points, the most of any region. Central and S. Asia: from 62% in 2000 to 92% in 2024. World: from 72% to 85%.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in DTP3 coverage in Central and Southern Asia, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2000 to 2024.

Data: WHO/UNICEF WUENIC, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_ACS_DTP3, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
