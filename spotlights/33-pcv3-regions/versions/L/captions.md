# Spotlight 33-L · PCV3 immunization, SDG regions compared: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2012, Central and Southern Asia has raised PCV3 coverage by 89 points, the most of any region. Central and S. Asia: from 0% in 2012 to 89% in 2024. World: from 19% to 67%.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates, not averages I computed from country values.

What lies behind the change in PCV3 coverage in Central and Southern Asia, and what could others learn from it?

Method: published aggregates, 2012 to 2024.

Data: WHO/UNICEF WUENIC, UN SDG Global Database (series SH_ACS_PCV3, extracted 1 Nov 2026), via the UN System Data Commons.
