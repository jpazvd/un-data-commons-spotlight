UN Data Commons Spotlight · SDG 3.b.1 · PCV3 coverage

Since 2012, Central and Southern Asia has raised PCV3 coverage by 89 points, the most of any region

Central and S. Asia: from 0% in 2012 to 89% in 2024. World: from 19% to 67%.

Data: WHO/UNICEF WUENIC via UN SDG Global Database, series SH_ACS_PCV3, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: PCV3, third dose of pneumococcal conjugate vaccine.
