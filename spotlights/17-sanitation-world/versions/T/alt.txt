Safely managed sanitation for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 6.9 points a year. So far it has risen 1.1 points a year, from 31.3% in 2000 to 58.5% in 2024. People using safely managed sanitation, the world: 31.3% in 2000, 58.5% in 2024. At that pace about 65.3% in 2030; reaching 100% needs 6.9 points a year.
