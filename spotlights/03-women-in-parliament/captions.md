# Spotlight 03 · Women in parliament (SDG 5.5.1): post captions

## LinkedIn

Women held 27.4% of seats in national parliaments worldwide in 2026, up from 13.3% in 2000.

The share has more than doubled since 2000, yet it is still just over a quarter. The highest shares in 2026 were in Rwanda (63.75%), Cuba (57.17%), Nicaragua (54.95%), Bolivia (50.77%) and Mexico (50.4%).

A statistician's note: this series is a count of seats, not a survey estimate, so it stays current: only 3 of 193 countries have a latest value from 2020 or earlier. SDG target 5.5 sets no number, so I show parity (50%) as a reference, not a target.

In your country, which single rule change (a quota, a party list rule, a reserved seat) has moved women's share of seats the most, and do the data show it held?

Method: share of seats held by women in lower or single chambers on 1 January, world series 2000 to 2026; country values are each country's latest year.

Data: Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU), Parline database, via the UN SDG Global Database (indicator 5.5.1) and the UN System Data Commons. Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
