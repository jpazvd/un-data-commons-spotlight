# Spotlight 22-L · Safely managed drinking water, Eastern and South-eastern Asia, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2000, safely managed drinking water in Eastern and South-eastern Asia has risen 7.1 points. From 68.2% in 2000 to 75.3% in 2024: 0.3 points a year.

In Eastern and South-eastern Asia, safely managed drinking water for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 4.1 points a year, against 0.3 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
