# Spotlight 22-G · Safely managed drinking water, Eastern and South-eastern Asia, by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Safely managed drinking water, E. and S.-E. Asia: 58.3% in rural areas, 85.1% in urban areas. Published aggregates for 2024: a gap of 26.7 points.

In Eastern and South-eastern Asia, safely managed drinking water for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 4.1 points a year, against 0.3 points a year since 2000.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published aggregates by group, not averages I computed from country values.

Method: published aggregates by group, latest year with both groups (2024).
