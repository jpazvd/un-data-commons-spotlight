In E. and S.-E. Asia, safely managed water for all by 2030 needs coverage to rise 4.1 points a year. So far it has risen 0.3 points a year, from 68.2% in 2000 to 75.3% in 2024. People using safely managed drinking water, Eastern and South-eastern Asia: 68.2% in 2000, 75.3% in 2024. At that pace about 77.1% in 2030; reaching 100% needs 4.1 points a year.
