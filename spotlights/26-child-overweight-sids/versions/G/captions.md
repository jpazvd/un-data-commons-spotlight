# Spotlight 26-G · Child overweight, Small island developing states (SIDS), by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

In 18 of 25 SIDS, child overweight was higher in urban than in rural areas in the latest survey. Latest surveys with both groups, 2010 to 2022; median gap 0.6 points.

A statistician's note: each country is compared with itself, within one survey, so this does not rest on comparing countries measured in different years. It tells you where the gap points, not how large it is: in 10 of the 25 countries the two groups are less than one point apart.

Where have you seen the gap in child overweight between rural areas and urban areas close, and how was it measured?

Method: latest year per country with both groups from the same survey (2010 to 2022); counts, not an average.

Data: WHO Global Health Observatory, UN System Data Commons (indicator NUT_ANTH_SH, extracted 1 Nov 2026).

Data and code: https://github.com/jpazvd/un-data-commons-spotlight
