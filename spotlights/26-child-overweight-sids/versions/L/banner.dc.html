UN Data Commons Spotlight SDG 2.2.2 · child overweight

Since 2000, child overweight in Small Island Developing States (SIDS) has risen 3.2 points

From 5.4% in 2000 to 8.6% in 2024: 0.1 points a year.

Data: UNICEF/WHO/World Bank JME via UN SDG Global Database, series SN_STA_OVWGT, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: SIDS, small island developing states.
