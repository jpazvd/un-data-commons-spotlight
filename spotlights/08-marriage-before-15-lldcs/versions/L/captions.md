# Spotlight 08-L · Child marriage before 15, Landlocked developing countries (LLDCs), by location: post captions

## LinkedIn

Since 2005, marriage before 15 in Landlocked developing countries (LLDCs) has fallen 4.6 points. From 13.3% in 2005 to 8.7% in 2025: 0.2 points a year.

In Landlocked developing countries (LLDCs), ending marriage before 15 by 2030 needs the share to fall 1.7 points a year, against 0.2 points a year since 2005.

A statistician's note: these are the custodian's published series. A single year can be revised when new data come in, so I read the direction over the whole period rather than year to year.
