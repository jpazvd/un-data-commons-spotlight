UN Data Commons Spotlight SDG 5.3.1 · marriage before 15

In LLDCs, ending marriage before 15 by 2030 needs the share to fall 1.7 points a year

So far it has fallen 0.2 points a year, from 13.3% in 2005 to 8.7% in 2025.

Data: UNICEF via UN SDG Global Database, series SP_DYN_MRBF15, extracted 1 Nov 2026 · Data and code: @jpazvd on GitHub
Notes: LLDCs, landlocked developing countries.
