# CCRR 2026 — COP31 social visuals

Images attached to five posts on UNICEF's *Children's Climate Risk Report 2026* scheduled around COP31
(Antalya, 9–20 November 2026). Hosted here so that Metricool can fetch them by URL.

| File | Post | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `ccrr_cop31_P1_opening.png` | Mon 9 Nov | Report Figure 2 (p.4), captured from the published PDF |
| `ccrr_cop31_P2_finance.png` | Fri 13 Nov | Banner generated by `ccrr_cop31_banners.py` (figures as printed in Chapters 5–6) |
| `ccrr_cop31_P3_education.png` | Sat 14 Nov | Banner generated by `ccrr_cop31_banners.py` (figures as printed in Chapters 2 and 4) |
| `ccrr_cop31_P4_implementation.png` | Thu 19 Nov | Report Map 1 (p.6), captured from the published PDF |
| `ccrr_cop31_P5_world_childrens_day.png` | Fri 20 Nov | Banner computed from `unicef/CCRR` pipeline data (`Country_level_hazard_exposure/Hazard_Population_Exposure.csv`) |

Source for every figure: UNICEF, *Children's Climate Risk Report 2026*, https://data.unicef.org/resources/childrens-climate-risk-report-2026/
(Global Child Hazard Database; WorldPop children's population layer).
